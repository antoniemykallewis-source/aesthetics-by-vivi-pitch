What we found / 1 of 3

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

01

No active ads found.

Meta’s selected practice page showed no active U.S. ads. If current, nearby prospects may meet competitors first. October 20, All ads/Active filters; other pages or countries may differ. See advertiser search ↗

02

Closing times differ by 2½ hours.

Google says Monday–Saturday until 8:30pm; your site says 6pm. Clients may expect an unavailable evening visit. October 20 comparison; confirm correct hours before changing either. See Google hours ↗

03

Your story is easy to miss.

The About page has a short introduction without video. New clients have less chance to hear your approach before choosing an injector. Page-specific gap; your social videos already exist. See About page ↗

What we found / 2 of 3

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

04

Review proof could be current.

The site says 250-plus five-star reviews; Google shows 5.0 from 332 total. Clear, current proof can help clients choose. October 20 snapshot; 250-plus can still be true. See Google listing ↗

05

Review cards repeat words.

The homepage repeats one review under different names. A possible copy error can weaken trust while someone chooses an injector. Verify the source; this does not establish fabricated reviews. See homepage ↗

06

Treatment proof misses the question.

The collagen-building injection page uses lip-treatment reviews. Someone considering a different service still lacks relevant reassurance before deciding to book. See treatment page ↗

What we found / 3 of 3

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

07

Social links leave people guessing.

Two Facebook addresses appear with a vague “Click here” label. Unclear links add friction when clients check your work. Both may reach one profile; no dead link established. See website links ↗

08

No clear route to ongoing advice.

The reviewed navigation has no blog link. Shareable treatment answers are harder to discover before someone is ready to book. A navigation gap, not proof that no articles exist. See navigation ↗
