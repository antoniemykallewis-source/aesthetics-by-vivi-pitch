Finding 1 of 3

Check each review label.

What we saw

Two home-page review cards repeat the same words under different names. This may be a copy error.

Why it matters

Readers need to know whose words they’re seeing.

Our fix

Check each source, then fix the names, quotes and links together.

Finding 2 of 3

Match reviews to the treatment.

What we saw

The Biostimulator page, for collagen-building injections, shows reviews about lip treatments.

Why it matters

Readers may still be unsure what to expect from this service.

Our fix

Add relevant, approved reviews or a short treatment explanation. Label general care reviews clearly.

Finding 3 of 3

What we saw

The site uses two Facebook addresses. They may lead to the same profile.

A vague “Click here” label leaves visitors guessing where it goes.

Our fix

Check the official profile, use one link and name the channel on the button.
